Most business intelligence tools require you to know what you're looking for before you start — you build a report, choose your filters, and hope the question you asked was the right one. OSARC's AI assistant works differently. Because every click, form answer, call transcript, and payment lives in one database, the AI has full context across your entire customer journey. You ask it a question in plain language, and it draws on the complete picture of your business to answer. No report building, no data exports, no waiting for a weekly analytics review.

## What the AI Knows

OSARC's AI has access to every data point your funnel generates across every native tool. That includes:

- Every ad click and UTM parameter, from the first time a lead touched your traffic
- Every form submission and individual answer, including fields that most CRMs never capture
- Every booking, reschedule, and cancellation, along with the time between form submission and call
- Every call recording and full transcript, for both setter and closer calls
- Every disposition logged by your sales team, including notes and outcomes
- Every payment and refund, tied back to the originating ad and funnel path
- Every workflow trigger and outcome, so the AI understands what automations fired and when
- Every email opened, clicked, or ignored across your follow-up sequences

Because all of this data lives in one system, the AI does not have to reconcile information from multiple sources. It reads one version of the truth.

## Asking Business Questions

You interact with OSARC's AI by typing questions in plain language — the same way you would ask a question to a chief of staff who had memorised every detail of your operation. Below are examples of the kinds of questions you can ask and the type of answer the AI returns.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='"Where did we lose money this week?"'>
    The AI reviews every lead that entered your funnel in the past seven days and traces each one through to their outcome. It surfaces the stages with the highest drop-off, identifies ad spend that generated no closed revenue, and highlights any setters or time slots with unusually low show rates — giving you a prioritised breakdown of where the leakage actually happened, not a generic funnel report.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Which setter had the lowest show rate?"'>
    The AI pulls every booking attributed to each setter over your chosen period, calculates the percentage who showed up for their call, and ranks the team accordingly. It can also surface contributing factors — for example, whether a setter's low show rate correlates with slow speed-to-lead times, or with a particular traffic source they were working that week.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"What do the leads who close have in common?"'>
    The AI cross-references the form answers, traffic sources, VSL watch times, and funnel paths of every lead who converted to a sale, then identifies the patterns that appear most frequently among buyers but not among leads who did not close. You get a profile of your best-fit lead — useful for refining your targeting, your form questions, and your qualification criteria.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Show me everything about [lead name] before I call."'>
    The AI retrieves the full history of a single lead: which ad they clicked, how long they watched your VSL, every answer they gave on the form, every previous call or contact attempt, any emails they opened, and any notes logged by your team. A closer can review this in under sixty seconds before picking up the phone — without opening a single other tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Show & Close Predictions

Before every booked call, OSARC's AI generates two predictions that your team can act on immediately.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Show Prediction" icon="calendar-check">
    The likelihood that the lead will actually attend the call. Calculated from their engagement history — how quickly they booked after submitting the form, whether they opened confirmation emails, their VSL watch time, and patterns from similar leads in your account who did or did not show up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Close Prediction" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    The likelihood that the lead will buy if they show up. Calculated from their funnel behaviour, their form answers, their traffic source, and how closely they match the profile of leads who have closed in the past.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Both predictions are visible on the lead record before the call begins. A setter can use them to prioritise which no-shows to chase first. A closer can use them to calibrate how much time to invest in a pre-call prep.

<Tip>
  Use show predictions to trigger automated reminder sequences for low-score leads — an extra SMS or email the morning of the call can meaningfully lift your show rate without any manual effort from your team.
</Tip>

<Note>
  AI predictions improve over time as more call outcomes are logged in your account. The more dispositions your team records — and the more accurately they record them — the sharper the predictions become. Accurate dispositions are the single highest-leverage habit your sales floor can build.
</Note>
