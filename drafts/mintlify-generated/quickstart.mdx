Getting your OSARC account fully operational takes four steps. By the end, your Meta ad account is connected and firing real cash events back as pixel data, your funnel is live and tracking every visitor, and your sales floor is active with your setters claiming leads and your closers taking booked calls. Work through each step in order — each one builds on the last.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In to Your Account">
    Go to [osarc.ai](https://osarc.ai) and enter the credentials you received during onboarding. If you haven't received your credentials yet, contact your onboarding contact directly — do not create a new account.

    Once you're signed in, take a moment to familiarise yourself with the main navigation. You'll see **Funnels**, **CRM**, **Analytics**, **Settings**, and the **AI Assistant** across the top bar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Your Meta Ad Account">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Meta**. Paste in your **Pixel ID** and your **Access Token** from your Meta Events Manager.

    The moment you save those credentials, OSARC begins tracking every visitor who arrives from your Meta ads — from first click through form submission, booking, call, and payment. Real cash-collected events will start firing back to Meta as pixel events automatically, with no additional configuration required.

    <Warning>
      Use a System User access token with full pixel permissions, not a personal user token. Personal tokens expire and will break your attribution tracking.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish Your First Funnel">
    Go to **Funnels → New Funnel** and choose a template that matches your funnel type — VSL funnel or webinar funnel. Then:

    1. Upload your VSL video to the native video hosting section of the funnel builder. OSARC will track drop-off per minute and correlate it with booking rate automatically.
    2. Add your application form using the native form builder. Every form answer feeds directly into your CRM and becomes available in your analytics.
    3. Connect your scheduler so prospects can book a call immediately after submitting their application.
    4. Click **Publish**. Your funnel is live and every event is being tracked against your Meta attribution.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate Your Sales Floor">
    Go to **CRM → Sales Floor** and complete the following:

    1. **Invite your team** — add your setters and closers by email. Assign each person their role.
    2. **Configure lead claiming rules** — set how leads are distributed when a new form submission comes in.
    3. **Set the claiming timer** — the default is five minutes. If a setter claims a lead and doesn't make a call within five minutes, the lead is released and the setter enters a ten-minute lockout.
    4. **Set up shift schedules** — define when your sales floor is active so leads are only distributed during active hours.

    Once your sales floor is active, every inbound lead is claimable by your setters, every call is recorded and transcribed automatically, and every disposition is logged in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Speed to lead is one of the highest-leverage variables in a call funnel. Aim for under five minutes between a lead submitting your form and a setter making the first call. OSARC's sales floor is designed to make that the default — not the exception.
</Tip>
