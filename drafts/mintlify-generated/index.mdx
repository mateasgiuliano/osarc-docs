## Getting Started with OSARC

Every OSARC customer goes through the same three-step journey from application to a fully live sales operation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply for the Pilot Programme">
    Fill out the short application at [osarc.ai](https://osarc.ai). Tell us about your funnel, your team, and your current monthly volume. We review every application personally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarding Call">
    Book a call with the founder. We'll show you your own numbers inside OSARC — attribution, show rates, close rates — before you commit to anything. If it's a fit, we onboard you hands-on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Your funnels, CRM, dialer, and ad tracking are all connected and writing to one database. Your sales floor is live, your setters are claiming leads, and you have full visibility from first click to collected cash.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  OSARC is built specifically for businesses running a VSL or webinar call funnel with a dedicated sales team, generating €30K or more per month. If that's you, every feature in this platform was designed around your exact workflow.
</Note>
