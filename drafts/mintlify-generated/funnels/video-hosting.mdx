OSARC hosts your Video Sales Letter (VSL) natively and links every second of watch time to lead outcomes. You can see exactly which minute people drop off — and what the ones who booked watched. Because your video player sits on the same database as your CRM and dialer, watch-time data is just another field on the lead record, available for filtering, segmenting, and reporting alongside form answers, call transcripts, and revenue.

## Uploading Your Video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Videos section">
    Go to **Videos** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Click **Upload Video** and select your VSL file. OSARC supports **MP4** and **MOV** formats. Processing time depends on file size; most uploads are ready within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title and thumbnail">
    Give your video a descriptive title so you can identify it in analytics later. Upload a custom thumbnail or let OSARC auto-generate one from the first frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embed the video in your funnel">
    Copy the generated embed code and paste it into your VSL funnel page in the funnel builder. Tracking activates automatically once the page is published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## VSL Analytics You Get

Once your VSL is live and receiving traffic, OSARC surfaces the following data in your **Videos → Analytics** dashboard:

- **Per-minute drop-off chart** — see the exact minute viewers stop watching, plotted as a percentage of total viewers who reached that point.
- **Watch time vs. booking rate** — compare the average watch percentage of leads who booked a call against those who didn't. Reveals the engagement threshold that predicts intent.
- **Average watch percentage by traffic source** — break down video engagement by ad, campaign, or traffic channel to understand which audiences engage most deeply with your offer.
- **Heatmap of most-rewatched segments** — highlights the moments viewers rewind and rewatch, which often signals the sections with the highest persuasive impact or the most confusion.

<Note>
  Video analytics link to the same lead records as your CRM and dialer. You can filter any CRM view by watch percentage — for example, show only leads who watched more than 70% of the VSL — and work that segment directly from the dialer queue.
</Note>

## Using Video Data to Improve Conversions

Raw view counts don't tell you much. The combination of drop-off data and downstream outcomes — bookings and closed deals — is where OSARC's native video hosting creates an edge over external video platforms.

**Diagnosing drop-off spikes**

If your per-minute chart shows a sharp drop-off at a specific point, review your script at that timestamp. Common causes include:

- A slow or unfocused hook in the opening two minutes
- A price reveal before sufficient value has been established
- A story or case study that runs longer than the audience's patience

Fix the script at the drop-off point and re-upload. OSARC keeps both versions in your library so you can compare performance over time.

**Finding your conversion threshold**

If leads who watch past 60% book at twice the rate of those who watch less, that 60% mark is your engagement threshold. Act on it:

- Add a direct call-to-action overlay or text prompt at that point in the video
- Tighten the first 60% of the script to reach that point faster
- Use the threshold as a lead scoring signal — leads past 60% can be prioritised in the dialer queue

**Matching creative to audience**

If one traffic source consistently produces lower average watch percentages than another, the issue is usually a mismatch between the ad creative and the VSL message. Use the by-source breakdown to identify which audiences are arriving with the wrong expectations before you spend more on that channel.
