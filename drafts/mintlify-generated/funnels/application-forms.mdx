OSARC's application forms collect information from prospects before they book a call. Because forms live in the same system as your CRM and dialer, every answer becomes a filterable field on the lead record — no copying data between tools, no Zapier, no broken syncs. When your closer opens a lead record five minutes before a call, every answer the prospect gave is already there, alongside their VSL watch time, ad source, and AI-generated show and close predictions.

## Building a Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Forms section">
    Go to **Forms** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new form">
    Click **New Form** and give it a name that identifies the funnel it belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your questions">
    Use the drag-and-drop builder to add questions. Choose a question type for each field — text, multiple choice, number, date, phone, or email. Reorder questions by dragging them up or down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set required and optional fields">
    Toggle each question as **Required** or **Optional**. Prospects cannot submit the form without completing required fields. Keep required questions to the ones that actually affect qualification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to your funnel and scheduler">
    Link the form to your application funnel page. Set the form's success action to redirect to your scheduler or confirmation page so prospects can book immediately after submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. The form is now live and writing submissions directly to your CRM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Question Types

OSARC's form builder supports the following question types. Each is stored as a typed field on the lead record, making it filterable and reportable in the CRM and analytics dashboards.

| Question Type | Best Used For |
|---|---|
| **Short text** | Names, company names, one-line answers |
| **Long text / paragraph** | Open-ended questions like "Describe your current situation" |
| **Multiple choice — single select** | Qualifying options like revenue range or team size |
| **Multiple choice — multi-select** | Scenarios where more than one answer applies |
| **Number** | Revenue figures, team headcount, ad spend |
| **Date** | Target launch dates, time-in-business questions |
| **Phone / email** | Contact capture; auto-formatted and validated |

## Per-Answer Analytics

OSARC tracks show rate and close rate broken down by each answer to each form question. You find this data under **Analytics → Form Insights**.

This means you can ask: of all the leads who answered "Yes" to question 3 versus "No", which group shows up to calls more often? Which group closes more often?

For example, suppose your form asks "Do you currently have a sales team?" If leads who answer "Yes" show at 80% and close at 35%, but leads who answer "No" show at 40% and close at 12%, you have two options:

- **Tighten your qualifier** — disqualify "No" answers at the form level so they cannot book
- **Adjust your confirmation sequence** — send "No" answers a different nurture path before the call to set better expectations

Per-answer analytics also help you identify which questions are worth asking at all. If the show rate and close rate are identical across all answers to a given question, that question is not predicting anything — consider replacing it with one that does.

<Tip>
  Ask qualifying questions that predict intent, not just demographics. "Do you currently have a sales team?" is more predictive than "What industry are you in?" The best qualifying questions surface commitment, urgency, and fit — not just category data.
</Tip>
