The OSARC dashboard is the command centre for your business. Because every tool in OSARC writes to the same database, the numbers you see here are always current, always consistent, and never require a manual sync from another tool.

## Dashboard Panels

The dashboard is divided into five panels, each giving you a different lens on your business. Every number pulls from the same underlying data — there is no reconciliation needed between what your CRM says and what your ad platform reports.

### Funnel Summary

The Funnel Summary panel shows the top-of-funnel and mid-funnel activity for your selected period. It displays:

- **Visitors** — total unique sessions across all your funnel pages
- **Opt-ins** — leads who completed the first step of your funnel
- **Applications** — leads who submitted a full application form
- **Bookings** — calls successfully scheduled from those applications

Use this panel to spot where volume is entering and where it is falling off. A high application count with a low booking count, for example, points to friction on your scheduler page.

### Sales Floor Today

The Sales Floor Today panel reflects live activity from your sales team. It updates throughout the day as setters and closers disposition calls. It shows:

- **Shows** — leads who attended their booked call
- **No-shows** — leads who had a call booked but did not attend
- **Closes** — calls that resulted in a confirmed sale
- **Cash collected** — total revenue recorded for the day

This panel resets at midnight in your account timezone. Check it throughout the day to see whether your sales floor is on pace.

### Attribution Snapshot

The Attribution Snapshot panel breaks down cash collected by the sources that generated it. It gives you a fast read on where your revenue is actually coming from without needing to open the full attribution report. The breakdown includes:

- **By ad** — which individual ads are producing paying customers
- **By creative** — which video or image assets are driving revenue
- **By funnel step** — which variant or version of your funnel is converting best

Click any row in this panel to jump directly to the full Attribution report filtered to that source.

### Team Performance

The Team Performance panel shows a ranked summary of your setters and closers for the selected period. For each team member, you can see:

- **Show rate** — the percentage of their leads or bookings that showed up
- **Close rate** — the percentage of shown calls that resulted in a sale
- **Speed to lead** — average minutes between a lead booking and the setter's first call attempt

Use this panel as an at-a-glance leaderboard. Click any team member's name to open their full individual performance breakdown.

### AI Predictions

The AI Predictions panel lists your upcoming booked calls alongside two scores generated by OSARC's AI assistant:

- **Show prediction** — the likelihood (expressed as a percentage) that the lead will attend their call
- **Close prediction** — the likelihood that, if the lead shows, they will buy

These scores are calculated from every data point OSARC holds on the lead: their form answers, VSL watch time, speed to lead, historical patterns from similar leads, and more.

<Tip>
  Start your day by reviewing the AI Predictions panel. Prioritise calling leads with high show predictions who haven't been contacted yet — these are your highest-leverage outreach opportunities before the call.
</Tip>

## Filtering Your Data

Every panel on the dashboard responds to the global filter bar at the top of the page. Adjust these filters to narrow or broaden what you are looking at.

**Date range** — Choose from preset windows or set a custom range:
- Today
- This week (Monday to today)
- This month
- Custom (pick any start and end date)

**Setter or closer** — Filter all panels down to a single team member. Use this when coaching a specific rep or reviewing their contribution to a period's revenue.

**Traffic source or campaign** — Isolate the dashboard to traffic from a specific UTM source, medium, or campaign name. Useful when evaluating a new ad campaign alongside your existing baseline.

**Funnel** — If you run multiple funnels inside OSARC (e.g. different offers, different audiences), select one here to see its performance in isolation.

## What Each Metric Means

The table below defines every metric that appears across the dashboard panels.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Show rate | Percentage of booked calls where the lead attended |
| Close rate | Percentage of shown calls where the lead bought |
| Speed to lead | Minutes from a lead submitting their booking to receiving their first setter call |
| Cash per lead | Total revenue for the period divided by the total number of leads generated |
| Booking rate | Percentage of completed application forms that resulted in a confirmed booking |
