Messages is the shared inbox. Every channel lands here, threaded by contact, so a conversation that starts on Instagram and moves to WhatsApp is still one conversation.

## Channels

Filter the inbox by **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**, **SMS**, **Email** or **Internal**. Internal messages are notes between team members about a contact. The contact never sees them.

## States

Each thread has a state:

- **Open**: needs attention.
- **Awaiting answer**: you replied, waiting on them.
- **Snoozed**: hidden until a time you pick (tomorrow 9am, next Monday 9am, or a custom time).
- **Resolved**: done.

Work the **Open** list down to zero.

## Replying

Type in the composer at the bottom of a thread. Pick the channel if the contact has more than one. Attach images or files. Saved replies live in **Quick Send** boards for the messages you send many times a day.

## Who sees what

Replies are tracked against the contact's owner, not the person who typed them. A manager covering a setter's inbox does not take their numbers.

## From the contact card

The same threads appear on the contact card, in the right rail, so you can message without leaving the record.
