title: "Finance"
description: "Cash in, by deal, by source, by period. Owner only."
---

Finance is the owner's view of money. It reads transactions as they arrive from Stripe and Revolut and matches each one to a deal.

## What you see

- **Cash collected** for the period, by closer, by product, by brand.
- **Payments**: every transaction with payer, amount, method and the deal it belongs to.
- **Renewals due**: deals reaching the end of their term.
- **Forecast**: expected cash from instalments and renewals already agreed.

## Matching payments

A payment is matched to a deal by the expected amount and timing, not by who paid. A partner or parent paying on someone's behalf is normal and matches correctly.

Payments that cannot be matched wait in a review list. Match them by hand from there.

## One source of truth per period

Historical revenue and live payment feeds cover different periods. Finance shows each period from its own source and never adds sources together, so totals are not double counted.

<Warning>
Finance is visible to the owner only. Managers see revenue in Sales, not the transaction level.
</Warning>
