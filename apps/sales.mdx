Sales is one app with several rooms. Which rooms you see depends on your role.

## The vocabulary first

- **Opportunity**: the card on the pipeline. One per person in the sales process.
- **Deal**: what was sold. A person can buy more than once off one card: an entry offer, then the main package, then a renewal.
- **Transaction**: one payment. Deposit, instalment, renewal, refund.

Keep these apart and the numbers stay honest.

## Pipeline

Cards move through stages. Drag a card or change its stage from the card. Marking a card won records a deal. Payments attach to the deal as transactions when they arrive from Stripe or Revolut.

## Appointments

Every booked call, with host, time, source and status. Open an appointment to edit it, reassign the host, or record the outcome.

### Call outcome

After a call, the closer records what happened: showed or not, sale or no sale, and a **grade** from 1 to 3 for the quality of the booking. Grades feed setter scoring.

## Numbers

- **My numbers** shows your own. **Team numbers** shows everyone (all roles can see team totals).
- **Show rate**: shows divided by booked calls.
- **Sets**: calls booked by setters, counted from actual bookings, never self-reported.
- **Leakage**: where revenue is lost between booking and cash.
- **Closer speed**: time from a booking to the closer's first touch.
- **Fresh leads**: new leads and how fast they were reached.

Every number opens. Click a metric to see which people are behind it.

## Client database and business income

Owners and managers get the client book and business income views, which read from the same deals and transactions as everything else.

<Tip>
Ask ARC instead of hunting: "how many calls did the team book today", "show rate per closer this month", "who has not been contacted since Monday".
</Tip>
