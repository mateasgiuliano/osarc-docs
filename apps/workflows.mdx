---
title: "Workflows"
description: "Automations you build: when this happens, do that."
---

A workflow is a trigger followed by actions. When the trigger fires, the actions run. You build them on a canvas.

## Triggers

Things that can start a workflow include:

- Appointment booked, rescheduled or cancelled
- Appointment status changes (showed, no show, sale, no sale)
- Form submitted
- Payment received, or a checkout abandoned
- Tag added to a contact
- New contact created

Most triggers take filters: any brand or one brand, any closer or one closer, any form or one form.

## Actions

Things a workflow can do include:

- Send a WhatsApp, SMS or email
- Wait for a period of time, or until a time of day
- Add or remove a tag
- Add a note to the contact
- Add the contact to an email list
- Activate an AI agent on the conversation
- Add the contact to another workflow

## Building one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">Open **Workflows**, click **New workflow**, and name it.</Step>
  <Step title="Pick the trigger">Choose what starts it and set its filters.</Step>
  <Step title="Add actions">Add steps in order. Add waits between messages.</Step>
  <Step title="Activate">Switch the workflow on. It stays off until you do.</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Every automated message in OSARC is a workflow step. If a message went out, there is a workflow that sent it, and you can open it.
- Workflows are yours. OSARC never turns one on for you.
- Booking reminders and the confirmation for an event live on the event itself, in Scheduler under **Notifications**. Follow-ups after the call are workflows.

<Tip>
Start with one workflow: a WhatsApp confirmation when an appointment is booked. Add a reminder the morning of the call once that works.
</Tip>
