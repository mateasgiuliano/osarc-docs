Team is the manager's view of the people. Managers and owners.

## Tabs

- **Performance**: per person, for the period. Setters: leads worked, calls booked, show rate on their sets. Closers: calls taken, shows, sales, cash.
- **Activity**: the live feed. New contact, call booked, stage change, outcome, sale.
- **Payout**: commission per person for the period, from the deals and transactions on record.
- **Settings**: seats, roles and access.

## Seats

Each person holds a seat: Call Setter, DM Setter, Closer, Coach, Manager, Owner. The seat carries the permissions. Someone can carry a second hat (a closer who also sets) and their numbers follow both.
