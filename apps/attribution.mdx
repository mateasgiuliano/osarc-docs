Attribution answers one question: which ad, page and channel produced this person, this call, this payment. It uses OSARC's own tracker on your pages, not the ad platform's report.

## How it works

Every funnel page carries a small tracker. It records a page view and an opt-in, and remembers the click that brought the visitor: the campaign, the ad, the source. When that visitor becomes a contact, the source is stamped on them. Every later booking and payment inherits it.

Leads that arrive straight from a Meta lead form are stamped from the form itself.

## Tabs

- **Overview**: leads, calls and cash by source for the period.
- **Live activity**: visits and opt-ins as they happen.
- **By channel**, **By ad / campaign**: the same numbers cut two ways.
- **Funnels**: per funnel and per step.
- **Leaks**: where visitors drop and where a source loses people before a booking.
- **Cohorts**: how a month of leads matured over time.
- **Compare models**: first touch against last touch against OSARC's default.
- **Identity merge**: when two visitors turn out to be one person.
- **Pixel events**: what was sent to Meta and when.
- **Phone closing**: sales closed by phone without a booking page.
- **Rules**, **Settings**, **Integrations**, **API and webhooks**: configuration.

## Cost per call

The sources panel shows spend against booked calls per source, so you see cost per call rather than cost per lead.
