---

Meta Ads reads your ad account and lines it up with what happened next in OSARC: leads, booked calls, shows, sales, cash. Managers and owners.

## Tabs

- **Overview**: spend, leads, cost per lead, cost per booked call, cost per sale for the period.
- **Campaigns**: every campaign, ad set and ad with spend and results. Sort by any column.
- **Events**: the conversion events Meta received from OSARC.
- **Financials**: spend against revenue for the period.
- **Countries**: where the spend went and what came back.
- **Strategies**: notes and plans per campaign.
- **ARC Analysis**: ARC's read of what is working and what to cut.
- **Attribution**: the same spend seen through OSARC's own tracking, not Meta's report.
- **Settings**: which ad account, which pixel, which events.

## Creatives

The creative tracker lists every ad creative with its spend and results, so you can see which hooks and videos hold up over time.

## The model

Under **Marketing**, the funnel model sets targets for each stage (cost per lead, booking rate, show rate, close rate) at three levels: best, flexible and intolerable. Actual numbers sit beside the targets.

## Tools

- **Budget calculator**: work backwards from a revenue target to daily spend.
- **Campaign builder**: draft a campaign structure.
- **Creative brief generator**: brief a new ad from what has worked.

<Note>
Meta's own numbers and OSARC's attribution will differ. Meta counts what its pixel saw. OSARC counts people who actually booked, showed and paid. See [Attribution](/apps/attribution).
</Note>
