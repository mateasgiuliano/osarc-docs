title: "Forms"
description: "Build application forms and surveys, embed them anywhere, and read answers on the contact."
---

Forms builds the questionnaires people fill in before they book: applications, surveys, intake forms. Each answer lands on the contact, so the closer reads it before the call.

## Build a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    Open **Forms** and click **New form**. Give it a name and choose the brand it belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    One question per screen, the way people expect. Short text, long text, choices, phone, email, number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add logic">
    Open the logic canvas to branch: send people down different paths based on an answer, or end early for people who do not qualify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Switch the form from **Draft** to **Published**. Drafts are not reachable by the public.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share or embed

Each published form has a public link at `osarc.ai/form/your-slug`. You can also embed it on any page, including funnel pages built in [Funnels](/apps/funnels).

## Results

The **Results** view lists every response with one column per question. Booked appears first, so you can see at a glance who went on to schedule. Open a row to jump to the contact.

## Forms and Scheduler together

The usual flow is form first, then booking. Scheduler can require an application before showing times, and grades the answers. See [Scheduler](/apps/scheduler).
