---
title: "Mail"
description: "Email inside OSARC, tied to the contact."
---

Mail is OSARC's email client. Inbound mail from contacts threads onto their record. Outbound mail you send from anywhere in OSARC (a contact card, Messages, a workflow) uses the same composer and the same sending identity.

## Sending

Open **Mail** and click **Compose**, or click **Email** from a contact card. Pick the from address, write, send. Sent mail appears on the contact's timeline.

## Formatting

Keep emails simple: short paragraphs, one link, plain signature. The composer formats for you. Avoid pasting from Word or Google Docs, which brings hidden formatting with it.

## Lists and campaigns

Contacts can be added to email lists from a workflow. Campaigns to a list go out through the same sending domain and land on each contact's timeline.

## Setting up

Owners connect the sending domain under [Integrations](/integrations/overview). Until a domain is verified, mail cannot be sent from OSARC.
