---
title: "Funnels"
description: "Public pages on your own domain: landing pages, video pages, booking pages, thank-you pages."
---

Funnels is where your public pages live. A funnel is an ordered set of steps: for example a landing page, an application form, a booking page and a confirmation.

## Steps

Open a funnel to see its steps down the left and the selected step on the right. Each step is one page with its own path, for example `/apply` or `/thank-you`.

You can run an A/B variation on a step. Traffic splits between the two versions and analytics shows both.

## Editing a page

Two ways:

- **Edit in the builder**: change copy, images, buttons and sections directly.
- **Ask ARC to edit**: describe the change ("make the headline shorter, move the video under it") and ARC edits the page. You see the result before it publishes.

## Bringing pages in

You can import a page you already have as HTML. OSARC keeps it exactly as it is and hosts it. Heavy images are downscaled and rehosted so the page stays fast.

## Domains

Connect a domain you own and point it at OSARC. One domain can serve many funnels by path: `yourdomain.com/offer-a` and `yourdomain.com/offer-b` can be different funnels. Owners and managers manage domains.

## Analytics and attribution

The **Analytics** tab shows views, opt-ins and bookings per step. The **Attribution** tab shows where the traffic came from. Both read the same tracker every page carries, so a booking is credited back to the ad and the page that produced it.

<Note>
Funnel pages fire two events: a page view and an opt-in. Bookings and payments are recorded by Scheduler and the payment integrations, not by the page.
</Note>
