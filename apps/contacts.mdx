Contacts is where every person lives. Leads, clients, past clients. Each contact card holds the whole story: source, form answers, bookings, messages, calls, payments and notes.

## The contact card

Open any contact to see:

- **ARC digest** at the top: a short read of who this is and what is going on, written from the record.
- **Timeline**: every touch in order. Messages, calls, bookings, payments, stage changes.
- **Details**: phone, email, source, owner, tags, brand.
- **Right rail**: quick actions. Message, call, book, add a note.

Messaging from the card writes to the same thread you see in [Messages](/apps/messages).

## Who owns a contact

Each contact can have a setter and a closer. Ownership decides who sees the contact when their role is scoped to their own leads. Booking a call for a closer assigns that closer to the contact.

## Finding people

Use the search at the top of Contacts, or Spotlight from anywhere. Search matches name, phone and email.

## Duplicates

OSARC merges duplicates carefully. When two records look like the same person but the evidence is not certain, they stay separate rather than being merged wrongly.

<Note>
Setters and callers see their own leads. Closers see the contacts assigned to them. Managers and owners see everyone. See [Roles](/team/roles).
</Note>
