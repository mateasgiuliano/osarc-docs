---
title: "Payments (Stripe and Revolut)"
description: "Connect payment providers so cash lands on the deal."
---

OSARC reads payments from Stripe and Revolut Merchant. Each payment becomes a transaction, matched to a deal and shown on the contact, in Sales and in Finance.

## Connect

Owners open **Integrations** and connect **Stripe**, **Revolut Merchant**, or both. Follow the sign-in for each provider.

## What happens after

- New payments appear within minutes.
- Each is matched to a deal by amount and timing. Third-party payers (a partner paying for someone) match correctly.
- Unmatched payments wait in Finance for a manual match.
- Payment events can trigger workflows: a welcome message on first payment, a nudge on an abandoned checkout.

## Checkout links

Payment links live with the offer. A checkout page inside a funnel sends the visitor to the right provider for their country and currency.

<Note>
Connecting a provider is the only setup. There are no keys to paste.
</Note>
