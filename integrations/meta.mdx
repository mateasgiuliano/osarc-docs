title: "Meta (Facebook and Instagram)"
description: "Connect your ad account, lead forms and Instagram inbox."
---

One Meta connection powers three things: ad reporting in [Meta Ads](/apps/meta-ads), lead forms flowing into Contacts, and Instagram messages in [Messages](/apps/messages).

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">Owners open **Integrations** from the dock and find **Meta Ads**.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Facebook">Approve the permissions. Pick the ad account and the Facebook page.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the pixel">Under Meta Ads **Settings**, pick the pixel and the events OSARC should send back.</Step>
</Steps>

## What flows in

- **Spend and results** for every campaign, ad set and ad.
- **Lead form submissions**, created as contacts within seconds and stamped with the ad that produced them.
- **Instagram DMs**, threaded in Messages.

## What flows out

OSARC sends conversion events back to Meta when a lead books, shows and pays, so Meta optimises towards people who actually buy, not people who click.

<Warning>
Only one system should answer an Instagram inbox. If another tool already replies to your DMs, leave OSARC's Instagram messaging off until you switch.
</Warning>
