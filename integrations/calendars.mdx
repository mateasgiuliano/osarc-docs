Some integrations belong to the business. These belong to you. Each team member connects their own.

## Your accounts

Open **Integrations** and look under **Your accounts**.

- **Google Calendar** or **Outlook / Microsoft 365 Calendar**: bookings made for you go on your calendar, and your busy times are blocked from the booking page.
- **Zoom**: each booking gets its own Zoom link, created for you.

## Why it matters

Scheduler shows a lead only the times you are actually free. Without a connected calendar, it only knows about bookings made in OSARC, and can double book you against meetings elsewhere.

## Business connections

Owners connect the ones the whole business shares:

- **Twilio**: outbound calls from the browser and SMS.
- **Resend**: the sending domain for [Mail](/apps/mail).
- **Google Drive and Sheets**, **Notion**, **Telegram**, **Discord**: for the apps that use them.

<Tip>
Connect your calendar on day one. It takes a minute and it is the single biggest cause of double bookings when missing.
</Tip>
