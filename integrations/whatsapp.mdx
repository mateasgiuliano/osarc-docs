title: "WhatsApp"

---

WhatsApp is the main channel for most teams. Connect a number and every conversation threads onto the contact, in [Messages](/apps/messages) and on the contact card.

## Connect a number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">Find **WhatsApp**.</Step>
  <Step title="Scan the code">Open WhatsApp on the phone that holds the number, go to **Linked devices**, and scan the code OSARC shows.</Step>
  <Step title="Wait for sync">Recent chats appear within a few minutes.</Step>
</Steps>

Once linked, the phone can be put away. OSARC keeps the connection.

## Team numbers and personal numbers

When you link a number you declare it **business** or **personal**.

- **Business**: the number your ads and pages advertise. Every chat is recorded, and a new person messaging it becomes a lead.
- **Personal**: your own phone. Only chats with people who are already contacts are recorded. Groups, unknown numbers and private chats never enter OSARC.

Only the person who linked a line can change its type. Not a manager, not the owner.

## Sending

Reply from Messages or the contact card. Workflows can send WhatsApp too, for confirmations and reminders. See [Workflows](/apps/workflows).

## If the connection drops

Open **Integrations**, find the number and click **Reconnect**. Do not unlink and relink from the phone unless OSARC asks you to.

<Warning>
A number that WhatsApp has banned cannot be reconnected. Use a different number.
</Warning>
