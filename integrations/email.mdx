---
title: "Email"
description: "Connect your own email sending account so Mail, campaigns and workflows send from your domain."
---

OSARC sends email through **Resend**, an email delivery service. You bring your own Resend account and your own domain. Every email your business sends (from Mail, a contact card, a campaign or a workflow) leaves on your account and arrives from your address.

Until this is connected, email from your business does not go out. OSARC never borrows another business's account to cover the gap.

<Note>
Owner only. It takes about 15 minutes, most of it waiting for your domain to verify.
</Note>

## What you need

- A domain you own, for example `yourbusiness.com`.
- Access to where that domain is managed (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, IONOS, Google Domains and so on).
- A free Resend account.

## Connect email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Resend account">
    Go to `resend.com` and sign up. The free plan is enough to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain in Resend">
    In Resend, open **Domains** and click **Add domain**. Enter your domain.

    <Tip>
    Many businesses use a subdomain such as `mail.yourbusiness.com`. It keeps your sending reputation separate from your everyday inbox.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the DNS records">
    Resend shows a short list of records (usually TXT and MX). Open your domain provider, find **DNS settings**, and add each record exactly as Resend shows it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the domain">
    Back in Resend, click **Verify**. It can take a few minutes, sometimes up to an hour. Wait until the domain shows **Verified**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    In Resend, open **API Keys** and click **Create API key**. Give it a name like `OSARC` and choose **Full access**. Copy the key. It starts with `re_` and Resend only shows it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into OSARC">
    In OSARC, open **Integrations**. Under **Sending identity**, find **Email sending** and click **Add**. Fill in:

    - **Resend API key**: the key you just copied.
    - **From address**: the address your emails arrive from, for example `team@yourbusiness.com`. It must be on the domain you verified.
    - **From name** (optional): the name people see, for example your business name.

    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card's dot turns gold. Email from your business now sends from your own address.

## Check it works

Open a contact with your own email address, click **Email**, and send yourself a short message. It should arrive within a minute, from the address you set.

## Changing or removing it

Open **Email sending** and click **Edit**. Your stored key is never shown again. Leave the key field blank to keep it, or paste a new one to replace it.

**Remove** stops email sending from your business straight away.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Card says **Still needed** | A required field is empty. Fill it and save. |
| Emails do not arrive | Check the domain shows **Verified** in Resend, and that the from address is on that domain. |
| Emails land in spam | Make sure every DNS record from Resend is added, including the optional one for DMARC. New domains build trust over the first few days. |
| You lost the API key | Create a new one in Resend and paste it in. The old one can be deleted in Resend. |
