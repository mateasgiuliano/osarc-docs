---
title: "Integrations"
description: "Connect the tools your business already uses. Two kinds: business connections and your accounts."
---

Open **Integrations** from the dock. There are two kinds of connection.

- **Business connections** belong to the business. The owner connects them once and every app uses them: Meta, WhatsApp business number, Stripe, Revolut, the email sending domain, Twilio.
- **Your accounts** belong to you. Each team member connects their own: Google or Outlook calendar, Zoom, a personal WhatsApp line.

Team members see only **Your accounts**. Owners see both.

## What connects to what

| Provider | What it does in OSARC | Who connects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meta Ads** | Ad spend and results, lead forms, Instagram DMs, conversion events back to Meta | Owner |
| **WhatsApp** | Conversations in Messages and on contacts, sends from workflows | Owner (business number), each member (personal line) |
| **Stripe** | Payments become transactions on deals | Owner |
| **Revolut Merchant** | Payments become transactions on deals | Owner |
| **Resend** | Sending domain for Mail and campaigns | Owner |
| **Twilio** | Calls from the browser, SMS | Owner |
| **Google Calendar** | Blocks busy times, receives bookings | Each member |
| **Outlook / Microsoft 365 Calendar** | Same as Google Calendar | Each member |
| **Zoom** | A meeting link per booking | Each member |
| **Google Drive and Sheets** | Files and sheets in Docs | Owner |
| **Notion**, **Telegram**, **Discord** | Used by specific apps | Owner |
| **GoHighLevel**, **Typeform** | Import contacts, forms and history from a previous system | Owner |
| **TikTok Ads** | Ad reporting (experimental) | Owner |

## Status

Each card shows **Connected** or not. If a connection drops, the card says so and offers **Reconnect**.

<Note>
You never paste keys or tokens. Every connection is a sign-in with the provider, and it belongs to your business alone.
</Note>
