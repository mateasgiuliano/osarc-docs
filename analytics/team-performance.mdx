OSARC's team performance reports give you a complete picture of how every setter and closer is performing, drawing from call dispositions, shift tracker data, and CRM outcomes. No manual spreadsheet, no guesswork about who is pulling their weight.

## Metrics Per Setter

Setters are measured on speed, volume, and the quality of the conversations they generate. The table below defines every metric tracked against each setter in OSARC.

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Speed to lead | Average minutes from a lead's booking submission to the setter's first call attempt |
| Show rate | Percentage of leads claimed by this setter that attended their booked call |
| Booking rate | Percentage of setter conversations that resulted in a confirmed booking for a closer |
| Talk time | Total hours spent in live calls during the selected period |
| Calls made | Total outbound calls dialled by the setter during the selected period |

Speed to lead is the single most important setter metric for show rate. The faster a setter reaches a new lead, the more likely that lead is to feel committed to the process and show up on the scheduled call. OSARC's 5-minute claim timer is designed to enforce urgency here.

## Metrics Per Closer

Closers are measured on attendance outcomes and revenue generated. Every closer metric in OSARC is derived from call dispositions entered in real time during or immediately after each call.

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Show rate | Percentage of booked calls assigned to this closer where the lead attended |
| Close rate | Percentage of shown calls that resulted in a confirmed sale |
| Cash collected | Total revenue attributed to deals closed by this closer during the period |
| Average deal value | Total cash collected divided by the number of closes |
| Talk time | Total hours spent in live closer calls during the selected period |

Cash collected is the definitive measure of closer performance. Close rate alone can be misleading — a closer taking smaller or easier deals may have a higher close rate while contributing less revenue than a closer working harder opportunities. Review cash collected and average deal value together.

## Using Team Data to Coach

The numbers in your team performance report are most valuable when used to ask the right diagnostic questions. The patterns below are the most common signals and what they usually indicate.

**A setter with high claims but a low show rate** suggests two possible causes. First, check their speed to lead: if they are claiming leads quickly but calling slowly, leads are going cold between the claim and the first dial. Second, review a sample of their call recordings — if they are reaching leads but the leads are not engaging, call quality or conversation framing may be the issue.

**A closer with a high show rate but a low close rate** is getting leads to show up but not converting them. This is almost always a skills issue rather than a lead quality issue. Pull their call recordings and listen for how they handle objections, specifically around price and timing. A targeted coaching session on two or three recurring objection patterns will typically move the needle faster than any other intervention.

**A setter with a low claims count** may be experiencing repeated lockouts from the 5-minute claim timer. If a setter claims a lead and does not make a call within 5 minutes, they lose the lead and enter a 10-minute lockout period. Frequent lockouts will suppress their total claims. Check the lockout log in their individual record to confirm whether this is the cause before addressing it in coaching.

## Accessing Reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team Report">
    Go to **Analytics → Team** in the left navigation. The report loads showing all team members for the current month by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Date Range">
    Use the date picker at the top of the page to set the period you want to review. Choose from presets (today, this week, this month) or enter a custom range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by Role">
    Use the **Role** filter to display setters only, closers only, or your full team side by side. This is useful when running separate setter and closer review sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drill Into an Individual">
    Click any team member's name to open their individual performance record. This view shows a full timeline of their calls, dispositions, lockout events, and shift data for the selected period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export if Needed">
    Select **Export to CSV** from the top-right menu to download the full team report. The CSV includes all visible columns and respects your current date range and role filter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Team performance data updates in real time as setters and closers disposition calls throughout the day. You do not need to refresh the page manually — the report reflects the current state of the sales floor at the moment you are viewing it.
</Note>
