OSARC is a marketing and sales operating system built from the ground up for businesses that run call funnels at scale. The problem it solves is straightforward: most businesses at €30K/month or more are running ten different tools — a funnel builder, a video host, a form tool, a scheduler, an ad tracker, a CRM, a dialer, an email platform, a workflow tool, and a reporting dashboard — and none of them talk to each other properly. Data leaks between every handoff. Attribution breaks. You can't tell which ad drove which call, or which form answer predicts a close. OSARC replaces that entire stack with one platform where every tool is native and every event — from the first ad click to a collected payment — writes to the same database.

## The Stack OSARC Replaces

OSARC is a direct replacement for all ten of the following tools:

- **Funnel builder** — Build and publish VSL funnels and webinar funnels natively inside OSARC
- **Video hosting** — Host your VSL and track drop-off per minute vs. booking rate without a third-party platform
- **Application forms** — Capture leads with fully native forms whose answers feed directly into your CRM and analytics
- **Scheduler** — Let prospects book calls on your setters' and closers' calendars without a separate booking tool
- **Ad tracking** — Track every visitor from first click through form submission, booking, call, and payment
- **CRM** — Manage every lead, contact, and deal in a CRM that shares the same database as every other tool
- **Dialer** — Your setters and closers call leads from inside OSARC — every call is recorded and transcribed automatically
- **Email** — Send automated and manual emails from the same platform that holds your lead data
- **Workflows** — Automate follow-ups, lead routing, notifications, and more without a third-party automation tool
- **Meta reporting** — See your real cash-collected numbers reported back to Meta as pixel events, not just lead events

## Who It's For

OSARC is built for businesses that are already winning — and need the infrastructure to scale without the operational chaos that comes with a patched-together tool stack. If you're running a VSL or webinar call funnel, you have a team of setters and closers working leads every day, and you're generating €30K or more per month, OSARC was designed around your exact workflow.

<Note>
  The ideal OSARC customer runs a VSL or webinar funnel, has a dedicated sales team with setters and closers, and is already doing €30K or more per month. If that describes your business, every feature in OSARC exists to solve a problem you've almost certainly already hit.
</Note>

## Who It's Not Yet For

<Warning>
  OSARC is not the right fit if you don't yet have a call funnel or a sales team. The platform is purpose-built for high-volume call funnel operations — it is not a general-purpose CRM or website builder. If you're pre-funnel or pre-team, OSARC will feel like more infrastructure than you need right now.
</Warning>
