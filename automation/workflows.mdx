OSARC workflows let you automate any follow-up action based on what happens to a lead. Because every event — a form submission, a no-show, a closed deal — is tracked natively, you can build sequences that react to real outcomes rather than relying on Zap tags that break when someone forgets to update them.

## How Workflows Work

Every workflow follows the same three-part model: a **trigger** starts the workflow, an optional **condition** filters which leads qualify, and an **action** defines what OSARC does next. Because every lead event lives in one database, you can build logic that would previously have required five connected tools.

- **Trigger**: The event that starts the workflow. Examples include Lead Booked, Call Dispositioned, and Payment Received. OSARC fires triggers automatically — no webhooks or manual tagging required.
- **Condition**: An optional filter that narrows which leads enter the workflow. For example, you can restrict a workflow to run only when the disposition equals No Show, or only when the lead source matches a specific Meta campaign.
- **Action**: What OSARC does when a lead enters the workflow. Actions include sending an email, sending an SMS, reassigning the lead to a different setter, updating the CRM status, or waiting a set number of hours before the next step.

You can chain as many conditions and actions as you need, and branch on different outcomes to build sequences that mirror your actual sales process.

## Common Workflow Recipes

The workflows below cover the most common automation needs in a call funnel business. Copy the logic directly or adjust the timing and messages to fit your process.

<Accordion title="No-show re-engagement">
  Automatically follow up with leads who miss their call, without any manual effort from your team.

  1. **Trigger**: Call Dispositioned → No Show
  2. **Wait**: 1 hour
  3. **Action**: Send email — "We missed you" (include a direct rebooking link)
  4. **Wait**: 24 hours
  5. **Action**: Send SMS reminder with booking link
  6. **Condition**: If no new booking logged after SMS
  7. **Action**: Update lead status to Nurture

  This sequence keeps the lead warm without tying up your setters on manual callbacks.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Speed-to-lead enforcement">
  Ensure every booked lead gets called within your target window. Slow speed-to-lead is one of the biggest silent killers of show rate.

  1. **Trigger**: Lead Booked
  2. **Wait**: 6 minutes
  3. **Condition**: If no outbound call logged against the lead
  4. **Action**: Send Slack alert to team manager with lead name and booking time
  5. **Action**: Flag lead as Uncalled in the CRM

  Pair this workflow with the shift tracker to hold setters accountable in real time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Post-sale onboarding">
  Kick off your onboarding process the moment a payment is collected — no manual hand-off required.

  1. **Trigger**: Payment Received
  2. **Action**: Send welcome email with onboarding instructions and next steps
  3. **Action**: Enrol lead in onboarding email sequence
  4. **Action**: Send internal notification to the assigned account manager

  Because the trigger fires on a real payment event (not a form fill or a tag), this workflow only runs for genuine buyers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Lead qualification follow-up">
  Re-engage leads who submitted your application form but did not book a call.

  1. **Trigger**: Form Submitted (no booking within 2 hours)
  2. **Wait**: 2 hours
  3. **Action**: Send SMS with direct booking link
  4. **Wait**: 48 hours
  5. **Condition**: If still no booking logged
  6. **Action**: Move lead status to Nurture

  This keeps qualified prospects moving toward a booked call without requiring manual outreach from your team.
</Accordion>

## Building a Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workflows builder">
    Go to **Automation → Workflows** in the left navigation panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new workflow">
    Click **New Workflow** and give it a descriptive name (for example, "No-show Re-engagement — Meta Leads").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a trigger event">
    Select a trigger event from the dropdown. Every native OSARC event is available here — Lead Booked, Form Submitted, Call Dispositioned, Payment Received, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions (optional)">
    Click **Add Condition** to filter which leads enter the workflow. You can filter by disposition type, lead source, funnel step, form answer, or any CRM field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add action steps">
    Click **Add Step** to build out your action sequence. Available actions include: send email, send SMS, wait a set time, update CRM status, reassign lead, and notify a team member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the workflow">
    Toggle the workflow to **Active** when you are ready to go live. OSARC will begin processing all matching leads from that moment forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Active workflows run on every lead that matches the trigger conditions, including your existing database. Test any new workflow on a small, filtered lead segment before enabling it across your full database to avoid sending unintended messages at scale.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Use the workflow run log at **Automation → Workflow Logs** to see exactly which leads entered a workflow, which step they are on, and what actions have been taken. This is your first stop when troubleshooting a sequence that is not behaving as expected.
</Tip>
