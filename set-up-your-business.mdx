A new business in OSARC starts empty on purpose. Nothing is borrowed from anyone else: no phone number, no email domain, no WhatsApp line, no AI account. You connect your own, and each channel switches on as you fill it in.

<Note>
This page is for the owner. If you were invited by an owner, start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart) instead.
</Note>

## Day one checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the link in your welcome email and set a password. You land on the desktop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Integrations** from the dock. At the top you see **Sending identity**: the accounts OSARC sends as on your behalf. Each card shows a status. A grey dot means not connected yet, a gold dot means live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an AI provider">
    On **AI providers**, click **Add** and paste one key: Anthropic or OpenAI. One is enough. This is what ARC thinks with, and your usage is billed to your own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect email">
    On **Email sending**, add your Resend account and the address your email comes from. Step by step: [Email](/integrations/email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp and Meta">
    On **Meta and WhatsApp**, add your WhatsApp Business line, and optionally your ad account. Then connect **Meta Ads** further down the page for ad reporting and lead forms. See [WhatsApp](/integrations/whatsapp) and [Meta](/integrations/meta).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect phone and SMS">
    Calls and texts are connected on the **Phone and Messaging** card further down Integrations. The **Phone and SMS** card at the top turns gold once it is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect payments">
    Under **Finance**, connect Stripe, Revolut Merchant or Whop so payments land on your deals. See [Payments](/integrations/payments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **Team** and invite each person with their role. Each of them connects their own calendar and Zoom under **Your accounts**. See [Invite and remove](/team/invite-and-remove).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You do not need everything on day one. Connect what you use. A channel you skip simply stays off: OSARC will not send on it until you add it.

## What stays off until it is connected

| Not connected | What does not work yet |
| --- | --- |
| **AI providers** | ARC, and anything that uses AI (summaries, drafts, the AI setter) |
| **Email sending** | Mail, email campaigns, email steps in workflows |
| **Meta and WhatsApp** | WhatsApp sends, ad reporting |
| **Phone and SMS** | Browser calling, SMS |
| **iMessage and SMS (Sendblue)** | iMessage sends. Optional, only if you use Sendblue. |

## Your keys are yours

- Every account you connect belongs to your business alone. No other business can see it or send on it.
- A key you paste is stored encrypted and is never shown again, not even to you. To change it, paste a new one.
- **Remove** on any card stops that channel straight away.
