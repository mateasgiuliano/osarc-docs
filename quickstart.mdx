---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Your first ten minutes in OSARC."
---

You need an invite email from your team's owner or manager. If you do not have one, ask them to add you under **Team**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invite">
    Open the link in the email and set a password. You land on the desktop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    Open **Integrations** from the dock, then **Your accounts**. Connect Google Calendar or Outlook, and Zoom. This is what stops double bookings. One minute, do it now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way around">
    The dock along the bottom holds your apps. **Launchpad** shows all of them. **Cmd+K** opens Spotlight from anywhere. See [The desktop](/desktop).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a contact">
    Open **Contacts**, search a name, open the card. Read the timeline from the bottom up: how they arrived, what was said, what was booked. Everything you do in OSARC ends up here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your numbers">
    Open **Sales**, then **My numbers**. This is your scoreboard. **Team numbers** is everyone's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the inbox">
    Open **Messages**. Filter to **Open**. Reply, snooze or resolve until the list is empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>
