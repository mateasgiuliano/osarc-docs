OSARC opens on a desktop, the way a computer does. Apps open as windows over it. You can have several open at once.

## Dock

The dock runs along the bottom. It holds the apps you use most. Click an icon to open the app, or to bring it forward if it is already open. Owners and managers decide which apps a role gets by default. You can rearrange yours.

## Launchpad

Launchpad is the full grid of every app you have access to. Open it from the dock or press **F4**.

## Windows

Each app opens in its own window. Move, resize, stack. Close a window and the app keeps its state for next time. Some actions open a second window on the right (a contact, a proposal, a file preview) so you keep your place.

## Spotlight

Press **Cmd+K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) from anywhere. Type an app name to open it, a person's name to find them, or a question for ARC. **Cmd+Enter** opens the same bar. See [Ask ARC](/arc/ask-arc).

## Quick add

Press **Cmd+N** to add something without opening an app: a contact, a note, a task.

## Top bar

Left to right: the ARC menu, the brand switcher if you belong to more than one business, then your notifications and the task centre on the right.

## Widgets and background

Widgets put live numbers on the desktop: today's calls, your numbers, the team's numbers. Add them from the widgets menu in the top bar. Change the background from the same place.

## Labs

Some apps are experimental. They stay hidden until you turn on **Labs** in **Settings**. Experimental apps work, but they change often and are not covered here in depth.

## Files and Trash

**Files** browses folders on your computer when you use the OSARC desktop app. **Trash** holds anything you delete inside OSARC until you empty it.

## Where OSARC runs

In the browser at `osarc.ai`, on your phone with the same apps in a mobile layout, and as a desktop app for Mac.
