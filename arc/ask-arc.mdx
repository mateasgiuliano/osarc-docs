---

ARC is the brain of OSARC. It reads every app, so you can ask it what you would otherwise go and look up.

## Open it

Press **Cmd+K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) anywhere. Type and press Enter. **Cmd+Shift+K** opens Thoughts, the full conversation window.

## What to ask

Anything. ARC is not a business assistant that only knows leads and revenue. Ask about an idea, a plan, a piece of writing, a training question. When you ask about the business, it reads live data:

- "How many calls did the team book today?"
- "Show rate per closer this month."
- "Who has not been contacted since Monday?"
- "What happened with Andrei Popescu?"
- "Which ad produced the most booked calls this week?"

Answers come with the numbers behind them. Ask "which ones" and ARC lists the people.

## Ask it to do things

"Move Andrei to Proposal sent." "Tag everyone who no-showed this week." "Rewrite the reminder for the strategy call so it sounds warmer."

ARC drafts a proposal for each. You approve it before anything changes. See [Proposals](/arc/proposals).

## Where else ARC appears

- **Contact cards** open with an ARC digest: who this is and what is going on, written from the record.
- **Meta Ads** has an **ARC Analysis** tab.
- **Funnels** lets you edit a page by describing the change.
- **Thoughts** is the full conversation window, with your history.

## Who can use it

Full ARC, with live business data and proposals, is available to the owner today. Team members get ARC's digests and analyses inside the apps. Wider access is a deliberate rollout, role by role, so that each person's ARC only sees what they see.
