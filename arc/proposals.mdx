---
title: "Proposals"
description: "ARC proposes. You approve. Nothing changes until you do."
---

ARC never changes your data on its own. When you ask it to do something, it drafts a **proposal**. You read it, then accept or decline.

## What a proposal looks like

The proposal opens in a panel on the right of the conversation. It shows:

- A title and a one-line summary.
- The **target**: which record, form, workflow or setting it touches.
- The **changes**, one by one, each with before and after.

At the bottom: **Decline** or **Accept and apply**. You can edit the changes before accepting.

## What happens when you accept

The changes are applied in order. If any step fails, nothing is committed, and the proposal shows what went wrong. Each applied change keeps a snapshot of what it replaced.

## What happens when you decline

Nothing changes. The proposal is kept as a record, the same as an accepted one.

## Why it works this way

One mental model for every change ARC makes: it proposes, you review on the right, you click accept. The same flow whether it is a grading rule on a form, a stage move on an opportunity, or a wording change in a workflow. Every change has a person behind it and a record of what was decided.

<Note>
Accepting a proposal is an owner action today.
</Note>
