---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The words OSARC uses, and what each one means."
---

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **App** | A tool on the desktop. Contacts, Sales, Scheduler and so on. Apps share one database and one brain. |
| **ARC** | The AI at the centre of OSARC. Answers questions, drafts proposals, watches for signals. |
| **Booked call** | Any appointment on the calendar, however it got there. |
| **Brand** | A business line inside OSARC. Switch brands from the top bar. |
| **Caller** | A team member who dials leads and books calls. A type of setter seat. |
| **Closer** | A team member who takes booked calls and sells. |
| **Coach** | A team member who delivers the service to clients. |
| **Contact** | One person. Everything that happens to them attaches here. |
| **Deal** | The package that was sold: what, for how long, at what price. Many deals can belong to one opportunity. |
| **Dock** | The bar of app icons on the desktop. |
| **Funnel** | A set of public pages (landing, form, booking, thank you) on a domain you own. |
| **Grade** | A 1 to 3 score on a booked call or application. 3 is best. |
| **Hook** | Something that runs when an event fires. Not a workflow. |
| **Labs** | A setting that reveals experimental apps. Off by default. |
| **Launchpad** | The full grid of apps you can open. |
| **Opportunity** | The card in a pipeline. The sales process for one person. |
| **Owner** | The person who owns the business in OSARC. Sees everything, including Finance and Brain. |
| **Pipeline** | A board of stages that opportunities move through. |
| **Proposal** | ARC's draft of a change. Nothing changes until you approve it. |
| **Set** | A call booked by a setter. |
| **Setter** | A team member who books calls, by phone or by message. |
| **Show** | The person attended the call. Show rate is shows divided by booked calls. |
| **Skill** | A capability an app gives ARC. |
| **Spotlight** | The search and ask bar. Open apps, find contacts, ask ARC. |
| **Transaction** | One movement of money: deposit, instalment, renewal, refund. Many per deal. |
| **Workflow** | A multi-step automation you build: a trigger, then actions. |
