The OSARC shift tracker gives you a live and historical view of your sales floor. See who is clocked in, how many calls they've made, and how their performance compares — all in one screen without pulling data from a separate tool. Because every call, claim, and disposition flows through OSARC, the shift tracker has a complete and accurate picture of what each setter and closer is doing at any given moment.

## What the Shift Tracker Shows

The shift tracker surfaces the metrics that matter most for running a high-performance call floor, updated in real time as your team works.

- **Who is currently clocked in**: A live list of every setter and closer on shift, with their clock-in time and current status (calling, idle, on a call).
- **Hours on shift and hours dialled**: Total shift duration versus the time actually spent dialling and on calls — so you can see if a setter is on shift but not active.
- **Total calls made, connected calls, and talk time**: The raw call volume, how many of those calls connected to a live person, and the total minutes of actual conversation.
- **Leads claimed and dispositions logged**: How many leads a setter has claimed this shift and whether every claimed lead has a disposition on record.
- **Show rate and close rate per setter**: Live numbers for the current shift and historical averages across all shifts. Spot who is consistently above or below the team baseline.
- **Speed to lead per setter**: The average time between a lead entering the queue and that setter making their first call — one of the most important indicators of setter discipline and show-rate performance.

## Clocking In and Out

Shift tracking is setter-driven. Setters start and end their own shifts, and all activity is automatically attributed to their open shift.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clock in">
    At the start of their shift, the setter opens OSARC and clicks **Clock In** in the top navigation bar or the Sales Floor screen. The shift starts recording from this moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the floor">
    All calls made, leads claimed, dispositions logged, and time spent in the dialer are automatically attributed to the setter's active shift. There is nothing to enable or track manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock out">
    At the end of the shift, the setter clicks **Clock Out**. OSARC finalises the shift record and calculates the summary metrics: total calls, talk time, speed to lead, and outcome rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the shift summary">
    The completed shift is saved to the **Team Performance** dashboard immediately. Managers can view it filtered by date, setter, or team — and setters can view their own shift history from their profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If a setter forgets to clock out, their shift remains open until a manager closes it manually from the **Live Floor** dashboard. Open the setter's row, click the options menu, and select **End Shift**. The end time will be recorded as the time of the last logged activity, not the time the manager closes it.
</Note>

## Manager View

Managers see the shift tracker through the **Live Floor** dashboard, which combines real-time status with the historical data needed to coach effectively.

**Real-time floor status**: The live dashboard shows every setter currently clocked in as a card or row, updated every few seconds. Each card shows current status (on a call, idle, in wrap-up), time since last call, and a live count of calls made and leads claimed this shift.

**Idle alerts**: You can configure an idle threshold — for example, if a setter has not made a call in more than fifteen minutes during an active shift, OSARC sends an alert to the manager and flags the setter's card on the dashboard. Navigate to **Settings → Sales Floor → Shift Tracker** to set your idle threshold.

**Historical reports**: Use the **Reports** tab within the Shift Tracker to pull data for any date range. Filter by individual setter, role (setter vs. closer), or the full team. Export to CSV for payroll, performance reviews, or commission calculations.

**Shift comparison**: Compare a setter's current shift metrics against their own historical average or against the team average. This makes performance conversations concrete — you're comparing numbers, not impressions.

<Tip>
  Use the **Speed to Lead** column alongside the **Show Rate** column when reviewing setter performance. A setter with a speed to lead consistently above ten minutes will almost always have a show rate below the team average — the data makes the coaching conversation straightforward. Start there before looking at call volume or talk time.
</Tip>
