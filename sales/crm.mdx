The OSARC CRM is the central record for every lead in your business. Because it shares a database with your funnels, dialer, and payment system, each lead record automatically contains the full picture — ads clicked, videos watched, form answers, calls made, and payments collected. There is no manual syncing, no export-and-import, and no data that lives in a separate tool. Everything is in one place the moment it happens.

## Lead Record Overview

Every lead in OSARC has a single record that grows automatically as they move through your funnel. When you open a lead, you see the complete picture without having to pull information from multiple screens.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contact Details" icon="user">
    Name, email address, and phone number captured at form submission. Editable if the lead provides updated information on a call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attribution Data" icon="crosshairs">
    The ad, creative, funnel step, and UTM parameters that brought the lead in. Written once at first click and never overwritten — you always know exactly what source produced the lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Form Answers" icon="clipboard-list">
    Every answer the lead submitted on your application or opt-in form, displayed in full. Use this to qualify the lead before the call and to filter CRM views by answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video Watch History" icon="video">
    How far the lead watched your VSL or webinar, broken down by minute. See exactly where they dropped off or if they watched to the end.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Booking & Appointment History" icon="calendar">
    Every booking the lead has made, including any cancellations or reschedules. Shows the scheduled time, assigned setter or closer, and current status.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Call Log" icon="phone">
    Every call made to the lead, with recording, transcript, disposition logged, and the setter who made the call. Recordings are playable directly from the record.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment & Deal Status" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    Revenue collected from the lead, deal stage, and any payment history. Updated automatically when a payment is recorded through OSARC.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Show / Close Prediction" icon="sparkles">
    OSARC's AI reads the lead's full history — form answers, watch time, call transcripts, and behaviour — and gives you a show prediction and a close prediction before every call.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Navigating the CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the CRM">
    Click **CRM** in the left navigation. The default view shows all leads sorted by most recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter">
    Use the search bar to find a lead by name, email, or phone number. Use the **Filters** panel to narrow by status, assigned setter, traffic source, date range, or any form answer field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a lead record">
    Click any lead row to open their full record. All sections — contact details, attribution, calls, payments — load on a single scrollable page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action">
    Open the **Actions** menu (top right of the lead record) to call the lead, send an email, reassign to a different setter or closer, or add an internal note. Every action is logged with a timestamp and the team member who performed it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lead Statuses

OSARC uses a default set of pipeline statuses to track where every lead stands. These statuses update automatically based on dialer dispositions, so your CRM reflects reality without manual updates from your team.

<Accordion title="New Lead">
  The lead has submitted a form but has not yet been called or booked. Newly claimed leads that have not reached a disposition remain here until the setter logs an outcome.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Booked">
  The lead has a confirmed appointment on the calendar. This status is set automatically when a booking is created through the scheduler.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Showed">
  The lead attended their scheduled call. Updated automatically when the closer dispositions the call as completed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="No Show">
  The lead had a booking but did not attend. Set by the closer when they disposition the call as a no-show, which can trigger a follow-up workflow automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Closed Won">
  The lead became a paying customer. Updated when a payment is recorded or when the closer marks the deal as won.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Closed Lost">
  The lead was not converted. The closer logs this disposition at the end of the sales call along with the reason, which feeds into your close-rate analytics.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Nurture">
  The lead is not ready to buy now but has not been disqualified. Leads in Nurture can be enrolled in email sequences or flagged for a follow-up call at a future date.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Use the filter combination **Status: Booked** + **Close Prediction: High** to build a prioritised call list before your shift starts. Your closers can spend the first minutes of their day on the leads most likely to convert.
</Tip>
