The OSARC pilot programme is how the first five businesses get access to the full platform. It exists because OSARC is purpose-built for a specific type of operation — a VSL or webinar call funnel with a real sales team — and the best way to prove it works is to set it up inside your actual business, with your real numbers, and show you the results before you commit to anything long-term. Every pilot member gets full platform access from day one, hands-on setup support from the founder, and a direct line for anything that comes up during the process.

## What You Get

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Full Platform Access" icon="unlock">

  <Card title="Hands-On Setup" icon="wrench">

  <Card title="Prove Your Numbers" icon="chart-line">

## What We're Looking For

Pilot spaces are limited to five businesses, and we're selective about who we bring in. You're a strong fit if:

- You're running a VSL or webinar call funnel as your primary acquisition channel
- You have a dedicated sales team — at minimum, setters making outbound calls to inbound leads
- You're generating €30K or more per month in collected revenue
- You're open to sharing your performance data so we can build the case study together
- You're willing to fully migrate your stack into OSARC — not run it alongside five other tools
- You want to move fast: from application to go-live in under two weeks

## How to Apply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill Out the Application">
    Go to [osarc.ai](https://osarc.ai) and complete the short application form. Tell us about your funnel, your team size, your current monthly volume, and the tools you're currently running. It takes less than five minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a Call">
    If your application looks like a fit, we'll invite you to book a call with the founder. On that call, we'll pull up your actual numbers inside OSARC — attribution gaps, show rate by form answer, cash collected by creative — so you can see exactly what you're currently missing. No pitch deck. Just your data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Onboarded">
    If it's a mutual fit, we start your onboarding immediately. Your stack migrates into OSARC, your Meta ad account connects, your sales floor goes live, and you're tracking everything from first click to collected cash — all within your first two weeks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only 5 pilot spaces are available. Applications are reviewed in the order they're received, and spaces are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis once we confirm a fit. If you're running the right type of business, don't wait.
</Note>
