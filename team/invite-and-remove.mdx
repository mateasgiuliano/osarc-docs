## Invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">Managers and owners open **Team**, then **Settings**.</Step>
  <Step title="Add a member">Enter their name and email. Choose the seat: Call Setter, DM Setter, Closer, Coach or Manager.</Step>
  <Step title="They accept">They receive an email with a link. Following it sets their password and lands them on the desktop.</Step>
</Steps>

If the email bounces or the link expires, resend the invite from the same place.

## First day

Ask new team members to:

1. Connect their calendar and Zoom under **Integrations**, **Your accounts**.
2. Open **Sales**, **My numbers**, so they know where their scoreboard is.
3. Read [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## Remove

When someone leaves, deactivate them. Their login stops working at once. Their history stays: every call, message and sale they were part of remains on the contacts and in the numbers.

Contacts they owned can be reassigned from **Team**, or left until a manager redistributes them.

<Warning>
People are never deleted. Deleting would erase history that other people's numbers depend on. Deactivate instead.
</Warning>
