OSARC is an AI-native operating system. ARC, the AI, is the brain. The apps are the tools that brain reaches for: Contacts, Sales, Scheduler, Forms, Funnels, Workflows, Messages, Mail, Meta Ads and more.

You work on a desktop. You open apps from the dock. You ask ARC for anything in plain language, and it answers from live data or proposes the change you asked for.

Every app shares one database and one brain. A booking made in Scheduler shows up on the contact, in Sales, in Messages and in Attribution without anything to connect.
